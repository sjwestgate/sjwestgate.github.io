I am a fourth-year MPhys student at the University of Manchester, UK. Welcome!

I am currently at JBCA thinking about cryogenics and transmon qubits. Before that I tried my hand at theory, deriving a framework to describe strongly coupled open quantum systems. I spent last summer playing with bubbles, and last year I was in College Park, MD, firing lasers at levitated nanoparticles. Next year I will be at CNRS cooling some optomechanical modes of a membrane, which is a project adjacent to the nanoparticle leviation.

Beyond physics I like to run, walk, play my violin, listen to music, read books and embroider. I hope to do some writing here about the various things I get up to!
